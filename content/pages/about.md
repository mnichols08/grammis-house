Family, my Greatest Blessing ❤️

Family has always been at the heart of my life. What began as young love with my childhood sweetheart has grown into nearly 55 years of marriage, four wonderful children, and eight beautiful grandchildren.

Our children are grown now, making their own memories and building lives of their own. Watching our family grow has been one of my greatest blessings.

Some of our sweetest memories have been made around the kitchen table, sharing favorite meals, holiday traditions, laughter, and love.
