This is the switch for the house's decorations.

**To choose the decorations yourself:** open **Settings** above, pick a season under **Theme**,
then **Save** and **Publish**. They stay up until you change them back.

**To let the calendar decide:** set **Theme** back to **Inherit**. Thanksgiving goes up
October 15, Christmas December 1, and they come down on their own.

The decorations on this page show what the whole house will look like.
