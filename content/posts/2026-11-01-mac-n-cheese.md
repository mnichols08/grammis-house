## Ingredients

- 1 pound medium shell pasta
- 1/2 cup butter
- 1/2 cup flour
- 4 cups milk
- 1 lb sharp cheddar, grated
- 1 lb extra sharp cheddar, grated
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 1/2 teaspoon black pepper

## Steps

1. Cook the pasta according to the box for al dente.
2. Melt the butter in a large saucepan over medium heat. Sprinkle in the flour and whisk often.
3. Slowly pour in about 2 cups of the milk while whisking constantly until smooth. Add the remaining milk.
4. Combine the sauce with the drained pasta.
5. Remove from the heat and stir in the salt, the pepper and about 3/4 of the cheese. Keep stirring until completely melted and smooth.
6. Place in a baking dish. Top with the remaining cheese and bake for 20 minutes at 375°F.
