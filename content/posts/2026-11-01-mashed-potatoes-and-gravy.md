## Ingredients

- 5 pound bag of potatoes
- 8 oz block of cream cheese, softened
- 2 boxes of chicken broth
- 1 stick of butter
- A dash of salt

## Steps

1. Peel the potatoes and cook them overnight in the slow cooker in the chicken broth. Add half of the stick of butter.
2. Once they're cooked, save the broth and set it aside for gravy later.
3. Mash the other half stick of butter, the cream cheese and the potatoes together.
