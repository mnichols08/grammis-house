## Ingredients

- 4 Russet potatoes, peeled and sliced in quarter inch slices
- 1 yellow onion, sliced into rings
- 3 tablespoons butter
- 3 tablespoons flour
- 2 cups milk, at room temperature
- 8 oz medium cheddar cheese, grated
- 1/2 teaspoon salt and pepper

## Steps

1. Preheat the oven to 400°F.
2. Lightly spray an 8x8 casserole dish with cooking spray.
3. Arrange the potatoes and onions in the dish, layering potatoes and then onions.
4. In a medium saucepan, melt the butter over medium heat. Mix in the flour and the salt and stir constantly with a whisk for 1 minute.
5. Add the milk and cook, stirring, until thick. Add the cheese and keep stirring until melted and smooth.
6. Pour the sauce over the potatoes and cover the dish with aluminum foil.
7. Bake for 1 hour to 1 1/2 hours, or until tender.
