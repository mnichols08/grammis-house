## Ingredients

- 1 bag of Pepperidge Farm stuffing
- 1 stick of butter
- 1 large yellow onion, chopped
- 1 cup chopped celery
- 2 tablespoons poultry seasoning
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 1/2 teaspoon pepper
- 1 package of Jimmy Dean sausage
- 2 1/2 cups milk

## Steps

1. Preheat the oven to 350°F.
2. Spray a 9x13 glass baking dish.
3. Melt the butter in a large skillet over medium heat, then add the onion and celery.
4. Season lightly with salt and pepper.
5. Sauté for 5-7 minutes.
6. Blend in the poultry seasoning, then pour the butter, celery and onion over the stuffing in a large bowl. Let it cool.
7. Add the raw sausage to the bowl.
8. Season lightly with salt and pepper.
9. Blend it together with your hands.
10. Slowly add the milk to the stuffing until it's even.
11. Put the stuffing mixture into the baking dish, cover with aluminum foil and bake for 40 minutes.
12. Uncover and keep baking for 15 minutes for a lightly crispy top.
